THE HOUSE THAT COUNTED • 6	
     	  				

5. THE RULE	

"What happens at thirty?" Mara asked.     	   		 
The woman placed a finger on the empty book. "A place is made. A	
name is removed. The house becomes quiet again."     	   		 

"How do I stop it?"	
     	  		  
"You must give it the number it has been waiting for."	
     	  	  	
Mara looked at the clocks. "Thirty?"	
"No. The first number."     	  			 
The woman opened the book. A single word appeared on the page,	
written in the handwriting from the letter: ELIAS.     	   	 	
Her father's name.	
Mara understood then. He had not been trying to bring her brother      			 	 
back. He had been trying to trade places with him. The house had	
accepted the bargain, but it had counted the wrong person. It had been      	     
counting the family ever since.	
From somewhere above them came three knocks. Then a child's voice:     	     	
	
"Mara? You took your time."
